The components

Each one enforces its rules on-chain. Together they form the system.

Marketplace

On-chain listings with prices pinned at purchase. Discovery, negotiation and hash-anchored metadata live here.

Escrow

PQ-gated settlement. Funds move only on the buyer's ML-DSA-87 signature, with credits-only payouts.

Oracle

DAO-governed verifiers attest fulfilment evidence. No single verifier can move an escrow alone.

Dispute board

Staked, randomly drawn resolver panels decide frozen escrows, with a real appeal to a larger panel.

DAO

Stake-weighted governance owns the verifier registry and protocol parameters, with a timelock and guardian veto.

AI assistant

Finds, negotiates and buys inside your mandate. Caps enforced in code. The model never moves money itself.
