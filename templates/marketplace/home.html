Six parts. One rule: the chain decides.

Marketplace

On-chain listings pin price plus a metadata hash at purchase. Discovery, negotiation, and AI drafts live here.

Escrow

PQ-gated settlement. Funds move only on the buyer's ML-DSA-87 signature, with credits-only payouts and per-escrow nonces.

Verifiers

A DAO-governed allowlist attests fulfilment evidence. An M-of-N threshold means no single verifier can block a refund.

Dispute board

Staked, randomly drawn resolver panels decide frozen escrows, with a real appeal to a larger panel that can slash the first one.

DAO

Stake-weighted governance owns the registry, vault, and board. Allowlisted calls, a 2-day timelock, and a guardian veto.

AI assistant

Finds, negotiates, and buys inside your mandate. Caps are enforced in code, the signed release binds recipient, amount, and nonce, and the model never moves money itself.
