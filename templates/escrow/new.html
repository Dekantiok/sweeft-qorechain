New escrow

Fund an escrow directly: no listing needed. Pay a freelancer, a seller, or anyone else: your QOR sits in the Vault until you sign the release with your ML-DSA-87 key (or the dispute board settles it). Your wallet must have a registered, proven post-quantum key.

Contract allows 1–90 days. Refund opens after it if nothing is proven delivered.

Sends the exact amount with `createEscrow`: the escrow is funded in the same transaction. Fees (2%) apply only on release, never on refund.

How settlement works
  • The seller marks delivered; a verifier or the carrier can attest fulfilment on-chain
  • You confirm receipt, then sign the release in QoreX: the Vault verifies the ML-DSA-87 signature with `pqcVerify`
  • Release credits the seller (2% fee to treasury/dispute pool); they withdraw from the escrow page
  • Either party can dispute: a staked resolver panel decides, with appeal
