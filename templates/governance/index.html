Sweeft DAO

Stake-weighted voting. A voter escrows QOR with their vote (minimum 0.01 QOR) and that amount is their voting power; the stake is locked until the proposal is final and can be withdrawn afterwards. Passed proposals execute on-chain after a 2-day timelock: the DAO owns the oracle verifier registry, so governance changes who may attest fulfilment.
