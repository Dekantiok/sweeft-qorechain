Dispute Board

Resolvers stake into a pool. After a dispute's join window, anyone draws a random 3-resolver panel (parties, the verifier and the carrier are excluded); a 2-of-3 majority records a decision. Either party may appeal to a 5-resolver panel (3-of-5, final) before the appeal window closes. Resolvers are paid a fixed fee from the raiser bond for either outcome.
