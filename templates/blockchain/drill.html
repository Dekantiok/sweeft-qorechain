Why a stolen key moves nothing

  • Release and refund need the buyer's ML-DSA-87 signature, verified on-chain by pqcVerify against the key registered with proof of possession.
  • The signed message binds escrow, recipient, amount, and nonce, so a signature cannot be replayed or retargeted.
  • Only authorized roles may act. A direct buyer or seller call cannot freeze an escrow without a bond.
  • Carrier proof binds escrow, carrier, status, and nonce. One verifier alone cannot block a refund.
